Determined to further expand my impact to students across the nation, I worked with two of my peers to develop a free, interactive teaching platform called Modeling and Visualizing STEM (MaVS at [mavs.uno](https://mavs.uno/)). From its inception, we had a clear mission: to interactively teach high-level STEM topics to anyone interested, using funding we then secured from the Suzanne and Walter Scott Foundation. This pursuit integrates my own passion for combining visual media, mathematics, and teaching. 

After [winning first place](https://some.3b1b.co/archive?category=non-video&year=2024&page=1) in Grant Sanderson's (3Blue1Brown) Summer of Math Exposition non-video category in 2024, we reached thousands of students across the world, bolstering STEM-related workforces with curious minds. Finally, we made the entire project free and open-source, just as I have since done with my entire FEM repository, reflecting my deep-seated belief that knowledge should be widely accessible for furthering scientific advancement.
